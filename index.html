Xuqi Zhang

Greeting! I'm a first-year PhD student, advised by Prof. Hannah Lu, majoring in Aerospace Engineering at UT Austin in Texas. My research interests lie in fluid mechanics and its wide-ranging applications. Currently, I focus on reservoir simulation, uncertainty quantification, surrogate modeling, and machine learning.

Prior to UT Austin, I began my research journey and obtained my master's degree in Mechanics at SUSTech in Shenzhen, where I was co-advised by Prof. Yu Liu and Prof. Lian Gan at Durham University. During my master’s studies, I also worked on experimental fluid mechanics (wind tunnel experiments) and acoustics.
